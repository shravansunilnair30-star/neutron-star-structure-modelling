VALIDATION VERDICT: NOT FULLY VALIDATED / NOT YET CV-READY AS A PHYSICAL SLy4 RESULT.
The maximum-mass agreement alone is not sufficient: the high-central-pressure part of this approximate EOS becomes acausal (c_s > c), including around the unrestricted sampled peak. The R_1.4 estimate also differs from the quoted reference by several percent. The central sound-speed check is a necessary but not sufficient causality check; a complete check must cover the EOS and each star's full interior. The low-density crust treatment and the use of a piecewise-polytropic fit instead of the tabulated SLy4 EOS contribute model discrepancy.

What is defensible now: the code solves the TOV equations for a specified piecewise-polytropic EOS, produces a reproducible mass-radius sequence, and passes the tested numerical integration sensitivity checks. It does NOT yet demonstrate a fully validated reproduction of the published SLy4 mass-radius curve or a physically valid maximum-mass prediction. Before claiming that, replace the approximate EOS with a vetted tabulated EOS, implement thermodynamically consistent interpolation, verify c_s <= c over the EOS and all stellar interiors, and benchmark against a published curve/table under matching conventions.
